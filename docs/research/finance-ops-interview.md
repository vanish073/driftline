# Finance-ops interview (required before Shift 7 / M4)

The brief: *"Find one person who does this job and ask them what their spreadsheet looks like before you build M4."*
Goal: about 30 minutes with someone who reconciles online payments (finance or ops at an online merchant, a D2C brand, or an aggregator).
Ask for a **redacted** screenshot or column list of their reconciliation sheet. Never take real customer data.

## Questions

**Their spreadsheet**
1. Walk me through your reconciliation sheet. What are the columns, and where does each one come from?
2. Which exports do you start from: the gateway dashboard, a bank statement, an order export? In what format (CSV, XLSX, PDF)?
3. How do you match a bank credit to payments when there is no reference? Do you use formulas, filters, or guesswork?

**Breaks**
4. Which mismatches take the most time? Rank: debited-no-order, late success, duplicate charge, short settlement, missing settlement, unknown credit.
5. How often does a settlement file or report arrive late? What do you do on those days?
6. Which deductions show up besides the fee and GST (holds, refunds netted, chargebacks, others)? Are they itemised?

**Time and money**
7. How many hours a week go into this? How many people?
8. Have you ever missed a refund deadline or paid compensation? How do you track the T+1 and T+5 deadlines today?
9. What does a "where is my money" ticket cost you in support time?

**Product**
10. When something needs a human, what evidence do you want attached?
11. If a tool did this automatically, what would you need to see before trusting it?
12. What would you want exported, and in which columns, so it fits your existing sheet?
